VERSION 2 — SIMPLE PHOTO REPLACEMENT
====================================

You do NOT need to change image code.

HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTOS
1. Open this website folder in Finder.
2. Open the images folder.
3. Replace the existing placeholder image with your own photo.
4. Your photo must have the exact same filename.
5. Choose Replace when your Mac asks.
6. Refresh Safari with Command + R.

HOMEPAGE:
Replace images/hero.jpeg with your own JPEG named hero.jpeg

ABOUT:
Replace images/about.jpg with your portrait named about.jpg

TRAVEL:
Replace the files inside images/travel:
travel-1.jpg through travel-6.jpg

EVENTS:
Replace the files inside images/events:
event-1.jpg through event-6.jpg

EDITORIAL:
Replace editorial-1.jpg through editorial-6.jpg

The homepage category cards automatically use:
travel-1.jpg
event-1.jpg
editorial-1.jpg

IMPORTANT:
Do not delete or rename the folders.
Do not change the filenames.
Use your own images with the same exact names.
Dragging and replacing pictures saves automatically.
